# Alexandria Server Guides

Start at the top and stop when you have what you need. Screenshots in these
guides come from a made-up demo library.

## Set Up

1. [Install Alexandria Server](install.md): the installer, where your data
   lives, upgrades, backups and restores.
2. [Put Alexandria Behind HTTPS](https-proxy.md): Caddy, nginx and Nginx
   Proxy Manager, plus every setting a proxy needs.
3. [Connect BOOX Tablets](boox.md): the BOOX sync services, PowerSync,
   enrolling and revoking tablets, and day-to-day BOOX use.
4. [Set Up AI Models](ai-models.md): every setting on **AI Tools and
   Recognition**, plus what to choose.
5. [Run Models on Your Own Hardware](local-models.md): vision, Whisper and
   embeddings on your own machine.

## Use

- [Using the Web App](web-app.md): a tour of the screens.
- [Recognize Handwriting](recognition.md): automatic recognition, single
  pages, whole libraries, and the Recognition Jobs page.
- [Search Your Library](search-and-tags.md): searching by word or by meaning,
  and BOOX tags.
- [Tasks and Calendar](tasks-and-calendar.md): both pages, and syncing them
  with CalDAV apps.
- [Turn Ink Into Tasks and Events](note-actions.md): red and sky blue pen
  colors that do things.
- [Audio Note Minutes](minutes.md): minutes from recordings and notes,
  sending them to BOOX, and typed-text notes.
- [Connect an AI Assistant (MCP)](assistants.md): Claude and other MCP
  clients.

## Help Out

- [Translate Alexandria](translating.md): choosing a language, and
  contributing a translation.
