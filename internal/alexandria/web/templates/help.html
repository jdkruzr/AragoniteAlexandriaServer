BOOX

{{th "BOOX tablets connect through the PowerSync app, which points the tablet's built-in Notes and NeoReader sync at this server instead of Onyx's cloud. Your notebooks keep their native format, and every tablet signed in to the same BOOX account shares one library."}}

{{th "Looking never changes anything. Opening notebooks, pages and books here doesn't touch your tablets. What does write back is always an action you confirm: renaming, moving or deleting notebooks and books, uploading books, sending minutes, refreshing reading data and recovering audio."}}

{{th "Page previews are drawn on the server from the ink files received so far. Pen textures are approximate. A missing preview does not mean the page is blank; some files may still be on their way."}}

{{th "When two tablets disagree, Alexandria follows the same winner the tablets' own sync chooses. Every version the server sees is kept, so earlier versions can be recovered, but changes a tablet never sent can't be recovered."}}

{{th "Reading shows books, highlights and bookmarks from NeoReader. Page numbers are shown exactly as the tablet recorded them. You can upload EPUB, PDF, MOBI or AZW3 books (up to 128 MiB); each tablet downloads a book when you open it in its library."}}

{{t "Pause, Cancel and Retry"}}
{{t "Pausing or cancelling stops results being published, but a request already sent may still finish and be billed. Resuming may send it again. Retry reuses unchanged successful results. Temporary failures retry automatically up to five times."}}
{{t "Image Size"}}
{{t "Each page is sent as a JPEG of at most 1 MB and 4096 pixels on its long side. Pages are never enlarged. Base64 encoding adds about a third to the request size."}}
{{t "Reprocessing"}}
{{t "Re-runs pages that were already recognized or requested, using the current settings. Unchanged pages reuse cached results; a new model or prompt can cost money again."}}
{{t "Connection Tests"}}
{{t "Send a synthetic image or sentence, never your notes. They may cost a fraction of a cent and don't save settings."}}

{{t "Audio Note Minutes"}}

{{th "Audio Note Minutes summarize a BOOX note from its recordings and its handwriting. Talk Insights on the device only hears the audio; here, what you wrote down counts as much as what was said."}}

{{t "What You Get"}}
{{t "The overall topic, 4–6 keywords, up to five key points (each marked as coming from the recording, your notes or both) and a conclusion. Each note also lists its recordings with start time and length, and the full timestamped transcripts."}}
{{t "How It Works"}}
{{th "Open a notebook and choose Summarize this note…. You'll see the recordings and how many pages have recognized text before anything is sent. Each recording is transcribed, then a summary model writes the minutes."}}
{{t "Pages Without Text"}}
{{t "You can include unrecognized pages; they're sent to your recognition provider first (charges may apply) and the minutes wait for them. Otherwise they're left out and listed."}}
{{t "Providers"}}
{{th "Set up in Settings → AI models under Audio note minutes. Transcription uses any OpenAI-compatible speech-to-text server, including a self-hosted Whisper. The summary model can be a local OpenAI-compatible server or an Anthropic model. Its instructions are editable there too; Alexandria always adds the response format it needs."}}
{{t "Keeping Up to Date"}}
{{t "Minutes are marked out of date when a recording, a recognized page or the summary instructions change. Updating is always your choice; unchanged recordings aren't transcribed again."}}
{{t "Where Results Go"}}
{{th "They stay on this server, appear in search and are available to MCP clients through get_note_minutes. Nothing is written to your BOOX until you choose Send to BOOX, which creates a separate text note named title_Minutes in the same folder. Your original notebook is never changed. Sending again replaces that note if it's untouched; if you've edited it, a new one is added instead. To send every new set of minutes automatically, choose that under When minutes are ready in Settings → AI models."}}
